## Snapshot and acceptance boundary

The ZIPs are frozen technical snapshots created before remote uploads. This receipt, download helpers and publication proof are later delivery records; they are intentionally outside those immutable archives. The technical snapshot records its external receipt as pending rather than predicting its own future transfer. This receipt closes that transfer check without changing any archive bytes.

Fleet operations and action records are synthetic; public EIA observations retain their separate attribution and retrospective availability restriction. No real-fleet saving or causal effect is claimed. User acceptance remains pending. No second project is activated.
