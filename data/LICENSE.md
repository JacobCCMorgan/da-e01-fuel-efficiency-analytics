To the extent copyright or related rights apply, the project contributors dedicate the original fictional sample datasets created for DA-E01 to the public domain under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode).

Provenance, origin labels and scenario definitions remain necessary for interpreting the datasets. CC0 is a reuse dedication, not a statement that any fictional person, vehicle or operating result exists.
